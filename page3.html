OUR STORY

It started with a loud idea.

Aetheros began in a borrowed garage in Columbia, South Carolina, where two USC students spent their weekends taking apart a broken lawn mower engine "just to see how it works." One wanted to build a business. The other wanted to build something that could talk to satellites. Somewhere between the spare parts and late-night pizza, they realized they wanted the same thing: to make flight more powerful and more possible for small teams with big ideas.

Today, Aetheros designs propulsion systems and engineering services for aircraft startups, university flight teams, and private builders. The garage is gone, but the loud ideas are still here.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Do you ship engines to dorm rooms?

Sadly, no. An AeroJet 200 is about the size of a small car and will not fit through a residence hall door. We ship to hangars, workshops, and airfields only.

Can I test drive an engine before I buy?

Yes, kind of. Our FlightLab Analysis service lets you run virtual airflow tests on your design before you commit, no runway required.

Is the electric IonCore E-1 really quiet?

Quiet enough that our team once ran it during a group meeting and nobody noticed. Results may vary.

Do you offer student discounts?

University flight teams get 15% off FlightLab Analysis. Reach out through the form above with your team name.
